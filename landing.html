Questions, answered.

How much does a turnkey venue cost? −

Depends on your floor area, the package and how far you take the fit-out. Send us your space and we'll come back with a proper quote and your free 3D design, usually within a few business days.

How long from signing to opening? −

Most open in 10–16 weeks. Because we run design, build and shipping on one clock, you skip the delays that come from herding separate vendors.

Can you customize to my space and brand? −

Always. Every package starts with a 3D design drawn around your actual space, theme and budget — branding, lighting and layout all in.

Do you install outside your home market? −

Yes — 2,000 venues in 100+ countries. We send engineers on-site and support commissioning remotely wherever you are.

What's the minimum space I need? +

Surprisingly little. A compact VR & digital-sports zone works from ~150㎡, and a boutique bowling club from ~250㎡. Tell us your floor area and we'll show you what fits.

Do you give an ROI or payback estimate? +

Yes. Alongside your free 3D design we model footfall, pricing and payback against venues like yours – most partners see payback in 10–16 months.

What does after-sales support include? +

Lifetime remote support, spare parts, software updates and on-site engineers in 100+ countries. If something breaks, we're on a call the same day.
